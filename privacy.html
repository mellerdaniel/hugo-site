Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

1. Scope

Hugo is a private application operated for a single household (“the operator”). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the operator. This policy describes how Hugo handles information obtained through Google APIs.

2. What Hugo accesses

With the operator's explicit authorization through Google's consent screen, Hugo may read calendar information (read-only). Only where the operator separately authorizes it, Hugo may also read mail, prepare mail drafts for the operator's review, and create or edit calendar events. Each permission is requested separately, only as needed, and can be revoked at any time.

3. How information is used

Solely to serve the operator: private summaries, reminders and drafts about the operator's own calendar and mail. Information is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to serving the operator.

4. How information is handled

Information is processed on equipment controlled by the operator and delivered only to the operator, through private channels. Third-party services may be used as processors on the operator's behalf — for example, to deliver a message or to generate a response — and only to that extent. Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with, or transferred to anyone other than the operator, and is not used for advertising or to build profiles. Authorization credentials are stored only on equipment controlled by the operator, with restricted access.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Hugo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Derived information is kept only as long as it is useful to the operator and can be deleted by the operator at any time. Access to Google data can be revoked at any moment at myaccount.google.com/permissions; revocation stops all further access immediately.

7. Children

Hugo is not directed at children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date at the top reflects the latest version.

9. Contact

Via the support email shown on the app's Google authorization screen.
