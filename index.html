Hugo

A private personal-assistant application. Not a public service.

What it is

Hugo is a small, private application used by a single household to keep everyday things organised — calendar, reminders, notes. It is not available to the public: there is nothing to download, sign up for, or buy.

Google services

Hugo connects to Google services only for the account of its operator, with that account's explicit authorization, to read calendar information (and, where separately enabled, mail) on the operator's behalf. What is accessed and how it is handled is described in the Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the account's Google permissions page.
